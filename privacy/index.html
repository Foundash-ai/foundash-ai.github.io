Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Foundash is run by Patrick Narcio in Northern Virginia.

What we collect

Only what you send us. When you ask for a free check, your own email app sends us your business name, your town and your email address. This website has no sign-up database, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

What we do with it

We use it to run your check and to email you the result. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts. When you visit, GitHub and Google receive the basic details any website visit sends, such as your IP address. Their privacy policies are at docs.github.com and policies.google.com.

Deleting your details

Email us and we'll delete what you sent.
